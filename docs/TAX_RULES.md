# Indian Investment Taxation Rules (Finance Act 2024 Audit)

## Statutory Overview

All tax calculations and displays across ArthaAtlas India reflect the enacted provisions of the **Finance (No. 2) Act 2024** (enacted into law following presidential assent on 16 August 2024, with capital gains amendments effective from **23 July 2024**).

---

## 1. Listed Equities & Equity Mutual Funds

- **Governing Statutes:** Section 112A, Section 111A, Section 10(38) (historical).
- **Qualification Criteria:** Domestic equities or mutual funds with &ge;65% domestic equity exposure.
- **Holding Period Threshold:** 12 Months.

| Classification | Statutory Rate | Exemption / Relief | Source Citation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG)** | **12.5%** | Up to ₹1,25,000 per financial year is 100% exempt. | Section 112A(2)(a) as amended by Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 |
| **Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG)** | **20.0%** | Normal slab deductions (Sec 80C/80D) not allowed against STCG. | Section 111A(1) as amended by Finance (No. 2) Act 2024 |
| **Dividend Income** | **Slab Rate** | TDS under Section 194 (equities) / 194K (MFs) at 10% if > ₹5,000. | Section 56(2)(i) read with Section 194K |

---

## 2. Specified Debt Mutual Funds (<35% Equity)

- **Governing Statute:** Section 50AA (introduced via Finance Act 2023, reaffirmed 2024).
- **Scope:** Any mutual fund unit acquired on or after 1 April 2023 where not more than 35% of total proceeds are invested in equity shares of domestic companies.
- **Deemed Status:** Irrespective of holding duration (even if held for 10+ years), gains are deemed as **Short-Term Capital Gains**.
- **Tax Rate:** Individual's applicable marginal income slab rate (up to 30% + surcharge + cess).
- **Indexation:** Explicitly denied ($0.0\%$).

---

## 3. Immovable Property (Real Estate)

- **Governing Statute:** Section 112 (First & Second Proviso).
- **Holding Period Threshold:** 24 Months.

### The Dual-Option Grandfathering Amendment (August 2024)
For properties acquired **prior to 23 July 2024**, resident individuals and HUFs are entitled to compute tax under two alternative methods and pay the lower of the two:
1. **New Regime:** 12.5% tax without Cost Inflation Indexation (CII).
2. **Old Regime (Grandfathered):** 20.0% tax with Cost Inflation Indexation (CII).

For properties acquired **on or after 23 July 2024**:
- Strictly 12.5% LTCG with zero indexation.

---

## 4. Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB)

- **Governing Statute:** Section 47(viic) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Redemption through RBI (Maturity / 5th-year window):** 100% Tax-Free for individuals.
- **Secondary Market Sale on Stock Exchanges:** Taxed under Section 112 at 12.5% LTCG after 12 months holding (as listed securities).
- **Semi-Annual 2.5% Interest:** Taxed under *Income from Other Sources* at marginal slab rates (no TDS).

---

## 5. Alternative Credit & P2P Lending

- **Income Classification:** *Income from Other Sources* (Section 56).
- **Tax Rate:** Individual's applicable marginal income slab rate.
- **TDS:** 10% TDS under Section 194A where applicable.
- **Loss Set-off Asymmetry:** Principal defaults on unsecured loans are considered bad debts of non-business individuals and **cannot be set off against salary, house property, or capital gains**.

---

## 6. Foreign Outward Remittances (LRS) & US Equities

- **Governing Statutes:** Section 206C(1G) (TCS) and Double Tax Avoidance Agreements (DTAA).
- **LRS TCS:** 20% on foreign outward remittances exceeding ₹7 Lakhs per financial year for investment purposes.
- **TCS Recovery:** Not an additional tax; adjustable as advance tax credit or refundable via annual ITR filing.
- **US Equities Holding Period:** 24 Months (unlisted shares under Indian tax definition).
- **LTCG Rate:** 12.5% without indexation.
- **Foreign Dividend Withholding (US):** 25% US withholding under US-India tax treaty; credited in India using **Form 67** under Section 90 (Foreign Tax Credit).
