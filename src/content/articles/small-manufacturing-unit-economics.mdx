## 1. Deconstructing the Financial Architecture of a Factory

Starting an industrial manufacturing enterprise in India requires understanding the distinction between **Capital Expenditure (Capex)** and **Working Capital (Opex)**.

A common failure mode for first-time entrepreneurs is allocating $100\%$ of their available capital to land, sheds, and imported machinery, leaving **zero liquidity for raw materials, power bills, and 60-day customer credit cycles**.

```mermaid

    TotalCapital[Total Enterprise Capital: ₹1,00,00,000] --> FixedCapex[Fixed Capex: ₹55,00,000 (Machinery, Shed, Electricals)]
    TotalCapital --> WorkingCap[Working Capital: ₹35,00,000 (Raw Materials, Credit Float)]
    TotalCapital --> EmergencyBuffer[Liquidity Contingency: ₹10,00,000 (3-Month Fixed Opex)]
```

## 2. A Real-World Financial Model: ₹1 Crore Small Engineering / Processing Unit

Let us model the complete unit economics of a specialized small manufacturing unit producing industrial packaging or machine components:

### A. Initial Capital Outlay (Capex)
- **Leased Industrial Shed Security Deposit:** ₹6,00,000
- **Primary Automated Machinery & Tooling:** ₹38,00,000
- **Power Connection (100 kVA HT transformer) + DG Genset:** ₹8,00,000
- **Internal Piping, Compressed Air, & Electrical Installation:** ₹4,50,000
- **Factory Licenses (Pollution Board, Factory Inspector, Fire NOC):** ₹2,50,000
- **Total Fixed Capex:** **₹59,00,000**

### B. Monthly Operating P&L at Full Steady State:
- **Monthly Revenue (at 80% capacity utilization):** **₹32,00,000**
- **Raw Material Costs (Bill of Materials @ 62%):** $-₹19,84,000$
- **Industrial Electricity & Power:** $-₹1,80,000$
- **Factory Floor Labor & Skilled Operators:** $-₹2,40,000$
- **Shed Rent (10,000 sq ft @ ₹25/sq ft):** $-₹2,50,000$
- **Consumables, Maintenance & Freight Outward:** $-₹1,20,000$
- **Administrative & Sales Overhead:** $-₹80,000$
- **Monthly Operating Profit (EBITDA):** **₹3,46,000 ($\mathbf{10.81\%  EBITDA Margin}$)**
- **Annual Operating EBITDA:** **₹41,52,000**

## 3. Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) Calculation

Assuming the entrepreneur funded the factory with **₹50 Lakhs equity** and a **₹50 Lakhs SIDBI / CGTMSE bank term loan** at $9.5\%$ interest:
- Annual Debt Service (Interest @ 9.5% on average balance): $-₹4,20,000$
- Statutory Machinery Depreciation ($15\%$ on WDV under Section 32): $-₹5,70,000$
- Profit Before Tax (PBT): ₹31,62,000
- Corporate Tax at $22\%$ (Section 115BAA): $-₹6,95,000$
- **Net Annual Post-Tax Profit (PAT):** **₹24,67,000**
- **True Free Cash Flow (PAT + Depreciation - Maintenance Capex):** **₹27,87,000**

### The Extraordinary Return on Equity:
$$
ROE = \frac{Net Annual Cashflow}{Initial Equity Invested} = \frac{₹27,87,000}{₹50,00,000} = \mathbf{55.74\% \text{ Cash-on-Cash Return!}}
$$

The entire initial equity investment of ₹50 Lakhs is **completely paid back in under 22 months of operation**, after which the factory generates over ₹27 Lakhs in unencumbered free cash flow every single year!
