## 1. Demystifying TDS: Withholding vs Final Tax Liability

A widespread myth among novice Indian savers is:

> *"The bank already deducted 10% TDS on my Fixed Deposit interest, so my tax liability is settled."*

This is dangerous and incorrect. **Tax Deducted at Source (TDS)** is simply an advance withholding mechanism enforced by the Income Tax Department to track financial transactions at origin.

If an investor falls in the $30\%$ tax slab:
- The bank deducts **$10\%$ TDS** under Section 194A.
- The investor still owes the remaining **$21.2\%$ tax** ($20\%  delta + 4\%  cess$) directly to the government via **Advance Tax** or self-assessment tax during ITR filing.
- Failure to pay this difference triggers mandatory penal interest under **Section 234B and Section 234C**!

## 2. Investment TDS & TCS Statutory Master Reference

| Statutory Section | Transaction Type | Threshold Exemption Limit | Standard TDS / TCS Rate | Higher Rate (No PAN) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 194A** | Bank Fixed Deposit & Recurring Deposit Interest | ₹40,000 per bank (₹50,000 for Senior Citizens) | $10\%$ | $20\%$ |
| **Section 194A** | Corporate NCD & Non-Bank Corporate FD Interest | ₹5,000 | $10\%$ | $20\%$ |
| **Section 194K** | Mutual Fund Dividend / IDCW Income | ₹5,000 per AMC per financial year | $10\%$ | $20\%$ |
| **Section 194-IA** | Sale of Immovable Physical Real Estate Property | ₹50,00,000 sale consideration | $1\%$ of gross sale value | $20\%$ |
| **Section 194DA** | Maturity payout of Life Insurance / Endowment policy | ₹1,00,000 aggregate payout | $5\%$ on the *net income* component | $20\%$ |
| **Section 206C(1G)** | Overseas Remittance under RBI LRS (US Stocks / FX) | ₹7,00,000 per financial year | **$20\%$ TCS** on amount exceeding ₹7L | Special rules apply |

## 3. How to Submit Form 15G and Form 15H to Stop TDS

If an investor's **total estimated annual income is below the taxable threshold** (e.g. ₹3 Lakhs under Old Regime or ₹7.75 Lakhs under New Regime with rebate):
- **Form 15G:** For resident individuals below 60 years of age and HUFs.
- **Form 15H:** For senior citizens aged 60 years and above.

### Crucial Filing Guidelines:
1. Must be submitted at the start of each financial year (April) to every bank where you maintain fixed deposits.
2. **False Declaration Penalty:** Submitting Form 15G/15H when your total taxable income actually exceeds the exemption limit is a punishable offence under **Section 277**, carrying potential imprisonment from 3 months to 7 years along with statutory fines!
3. Always verify that deducted TDS appears accurately in your **Annual Information Statement (AIS)** and **Form 26AS** on the Income Tax Portal.
